The resource-favoriting functionality makes it easy to keep your most-used resources just a click away. Whether you’re working with data, building apps, or queries, you can now mark key items as favorites for instant access, no more hunting through menus.

## What can be favourited?

* Datasources
  * Tables
  * Views
  * Custom queries
  * REST requests
* Automations
* Apps

When you first start, the area where these are favoured will be blank.

## How it works

Favouriting is designed to be fast and straightforward:

1. Hover over any in the above list\
   When hovered, each eligible item has a star icon next to it. Click the star to add it to your favourites.
2. Once you favourite an item, it appears in your Favourites menu and stays accessible across your workspace. Favourites are user-specific so that each user can have their own set.
3. Unfavourite Anytime\
   Need to clean up your list? Just click the star again to remove an item from your favourites.
