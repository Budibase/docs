Whether you are cloud or self-hosting, you will need to log-in to the **online** account portal to [upgrade](doc:usage) your workspaces or close your account.

Follow this link to access the account portal: [https://account.budibase.app/portal/account](https://account.budibase.app/portal/account)

### Password reset

If you signed up to a Budibase Account with an email/password, and not SSO, then you will have the options to change your account holder password.

<Image align="center" border={false} src="https://files.readme.io/fe2ccbd5b6fd8b168451278daf033c1b636fc19c7a23f8fad05bd62abe5d4b17-reset_account_password.png" />

### Account deletion

1. Login to the account you wish to delete.
2. Click the `Settings` navigation item at the top of your page.
   ![](https://files.readme.io/0e37de43f70063520cdb10982c725ae9ec15d2b36b65392ba20289da629718f6-Screenshot_2025-09-29_at_13.38.21.png)
3. Click the `Upgrade` button. This should redirect you to your plans available.
   ![](https://files.readme.io/8421ec93b14fd928acbb20a1bdd26e0fbcbf6c1ec68455ba2bef73e73fa8269c-Screenshot_2025-09-29_at_13.38.43.png)
4. Click the navigation item `Account` within the navigation bar.
5. Click the `Deleted Account` button which is red and go through the confirmation window. (Your account will successfully be deleted. **This is permanent and irreversible**.)
   ![](https://files.readme.io/70e0798-Screenshot_2023-11-10_at_12.43.53.png)
