The `link` component provides a text-based way for users to natigate to a new screen in your app, or elsewhere on the internet altogether.

## Adding a link

To add the `link` element, click `Basic > Link`.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/d0ebb8189ede0d9854b9c664c71bf22f7696da04c1f9ea8c0764a6d0055acf95-Screenshot_2025-09-04_at_14.23.13.png" />

After adding the link there's two settings in the <Glossary>Settings Panel</Glossary> you will want to change. The `URL`, or the location to which you want to link, and if the link should open in a new tab or not with the `New Tab` checkbox.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/ec3bfdc413e9b361930eaa53b7cb9993fd0ee33ca260e91247600130ca7938d7-Screenshot_2025-09-04_at_16.26.49.png" />

The Link component can be useful for making a footer for the bottom of your screens.
